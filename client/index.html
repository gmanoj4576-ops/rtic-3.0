About The Challenge

Bridging the Gap Between Technology & Rural Ecosystems

The Rural Tech Innovation Challenge (RTIC) 3.0 is a national-level initiative designed to bring technological interventions to India's rural areas. Participants are encouraged to submit hardware and software solutions focused on improving agricultural yields, rural electrification, healthcare delivery, clean water supply, and waste management.

Organized in association with IEEE, RTIC provides a competitive platform for student developers, engineers, and tech-enthusiasts to build working prototypes, undergo professional mentorship, and present solutions to industry experts and venture capital partners.

Rules & Guidelines

Event Participation Rules

  • Only the Team Leader can create and submit the team registration.
  • Each team can contain 1 to 4 members (1 Leader, and up to 3 optional members). Solo/single-member registrations are fully allowed!
  • A registration fee of ₹350 per member is mandatory (calculated dynamically based on your team size).
  • All team details, payment transaction IDs, and receipt screenshots are verified by the admin panel. Incorrect/duplicate details will trigger a rejection.
